How to Play

Learn the interface and get playing fast.

Use this guide to move through games, load PGN, and understand the AI coach.

Start Playing Go Back Home

1. Start with Analysis

Use the Analysis mode to explore positions, load games, and ask the AI coach questions.

2. Use the new move buttons

Tap the Previous and Next buttons to step through the game history one move at a time.

3. Try the coach

Tap Explain Move, Best Move, or type a question in chat. In Play AI, the coach can also provide automatic hints after the opponent moves.

4. Play AI safely

Switch to Play AI when you want a fresh challenge. If you have unsaved analysis moves, the app will warn you with a page UI before switching.

5. Save or share your games

Save a game to the library, then reopen it from Saved Games. Each saved entry includes a board preview of the final position.
